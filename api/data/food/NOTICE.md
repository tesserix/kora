# Food data attribution

Kora adapts public food composition and packaged-product datasets into a
per-100 g nutrition index. Source values, estimates and serving conventions
are retained where available; unavailable nutrients are omitted. Recipe rows
are calculations from ingredients and cooked yields, not laboratory analyses.

- AFCD Release 3 and AUSNUT 2023: © Food Standards Australia New Zealand.
  [FSANZ food databases](https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/science-data/food-nutrient-databases).
- USDA FoodData Central, SR Legacy and FNDDS: public domain.
  [FoodData Central](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/).
- Open Food Facts: [Open Database License](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/).
  [Original database](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/). The country snapshots
  are adapted, filtered extracts. Public distribution of an OFF-derived
  database remains subject to ODbL's attribution and share-alike terms.
- IFCT 2017: National Institute of Nutrition, Hyderabad, through the
  [compositions transcription at tag 2.0.5](https://github.com/ifct2017/compositions/tree/2.0.5).
  The transcription's MIT licence is reproduced below. Later tags and HEAD
  are not covered by this permission.
