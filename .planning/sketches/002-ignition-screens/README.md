# Sketch 002: Ignition Screens

## Design Question
Sketch 001 settled the language: fused bezel clusters, engraved zone rules, recessed
wells, warm lume, motion-on-entry, and the notched dock. This sketch proves the
language generalizes — the tabs are the three remaining main screens rather than
competing variants.

## How to View
open .planning/sketches/002-ignition-screens/index.html

## Screens
- **Diary** — the whole day is one instrument: day-total (animated track), per-slot zones with engraved subtotals, add-dinner ghost, and water in the recessed footer well; the week strip becomes a machined rail with glowing goal pips.
- **Trends** — weight is the hero cluster (self-drawing line, lume endpoint, live segmented control); energy bars grow on entry with orange reserved for over-budget days; streak/sleep as sheened glass tiles.
- **More** — only the identity hero earns the full bezel (avatar well with a whisper of orange lume); settings groups stay on lighter glass with recessed icon wells; teal used strictly for status.

## What to Look For
- Hierarchy discipline: exactly one bezel-grade instrument per screen — does that rule hold up?
- Motion continuity: track fill / chart draw-in / bar growth all echo the home ignition sweep.
- Accent budget per screen (pips+track on Diary; endpoint+over-budget on Trends; avatar lume on More).
- Dock v2 everywhere with the correct active tab.
