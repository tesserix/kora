Circular ring gauge — the calorie/macro ring used across Kora. Center content is passed as children.

```jsx
<CircularProgress value={1420} max={2000} size={120} color="var(--primary)">
  <strong style={{fontSize:24}}>1420</strong>
  <span style={{fontSize:12,color:"var(--muted-foreground)"}}>kcal</span>
</CircularProgress>
```
